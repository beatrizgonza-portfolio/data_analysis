



/Key Insights & Conclusion
Impact of Attendance on Student Performance:Students with regular attendance ($\ge 75\%$) achieve an average exam score of 68.6, compared to 64.8 for students in the at-risk group ($<75\%$ attendance) — representing a statistically relevant drop of nearly 4 percentage points in overall academic performance.
Volumetric Risk Analysis:Out of the total 6,607 students analyzed, 36.3% (2,396 students) fall into the "At-Risk" attendance category. This high volume indicates that low attendance is a widespread issue rather than an isolated edge case.
